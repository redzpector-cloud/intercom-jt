V1.6.3 – Wi-Fi Direct discovery stabilization

- Stop existing peer discovery before starting a new discovery cycle to reduce BUSY failures.
- Retry discovery automatically with controlled timing.
- Check Wi-Fi is enabled.
- Check Location services are enabled because some Android devices require Location for Wi-Fi P2P discovery.
- Show readable discovery failure reason.
- Request peers immediately after successful discovery.
- Manual CARI & HUBUNGKAN now triggers discovery + peer request.
- Removed recursive peer polling that could create overlapping discovery calls.
- Audio architecture unchanged.
